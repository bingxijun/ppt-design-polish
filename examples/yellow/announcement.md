# ATELIER NORTH studio brief

ATELIER NORTH is a fictional architecture studio. All projects, numbers and images in this example are illustrative. Images are AI-generated.

Spaces for better days. We design calm workspaces and small cultural buildings through careful use of daylight, tactile materials and a clear floor plan. Our approach is design with room to breathe.

The pavilion concept pairs pale limestone with a narrow yellow entrance and an olive courtyard. Material warmth and the rhythm of light define the space. This fictional concept illustrates an architectural direction, not a completed building.

Our demonstration portfolio contains 24 projects: 12 workplaces, 8 cultural spaces and 4 private residences. These are illustrative counts, not business results.

Our process has four stages: Discover, Design, Develop and Deliver. Discover clarifies the brief, Design tests spatial ideas, Develop resolves materials and details, and Deliver supports the agreed handover. No project duration or fee is specified.
