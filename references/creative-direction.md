# Creative direction / 创意与审美决策

Read for a full new deck/redesign when the user wants originality, artistry, or a polished visual identity, or when the visual direction is still unresolved. Follow an already chosen brand/reference; a small correction does not need concept exploration.

用于完整新稿或重设计中明确要求创意、艺术感、精致视觉，或视觉方向尚未确定的任务。已选定的品牌与参考继续沿用，简单改字无需重新探索。

## Make the brief concrete / 将审美愿望转成设计目标

Translate “clean, premium, creative” into decisions for this audience: the information they should notice first, the intended feeling, the evidence that makes this subject specific, and the visual conventions worth retaining. “Premium” may mean confident typography and measured space in a keynote; a research deck may need precise figure annotation and a disciplined reading grid.

将“整洁、高级、有创意”转成当前受众需要的结果：先看到什么、产生怎样的感受、哪些证据让主题具体、哪些行业惯例值得保留。发布演讲可侧重字体张力与空间，研究汇报可侧重精确图注与阅读秩序。“高级”没有固定配色或字体。

Describe a visual concept in one actionable sentence that connects subject and composition. For a daylight lesson: “Let light paths organize the diagrams and reading order.” The concept should guide crop, type placement, diagrams, and pacing. A label such as “minimal luxury” alone does not tell the builder what to do.

用一句可执行的话连接主题与构图。例如日光教学：“让光的路径组织图解与阅读顺序。”概念应影响裁切、字体位置、图解和节奏。只写“极简奢华”不足以指导制作。

## Explore composition before scaling / 扩稿前比较构图

When the direction is open and creativity matters, compare a small number of substantially different approaches in the workspace, usually two or three. Vary spatial organization, typographic hierarchy, image treatment, or explanatory form. Changing only colors is one direction with variants. Keep facts and slide purpose fixed so the comparison is useful. A short concept sketch can discard a weak route; build rendered candidates when the decision depends on visual judgment.

方向开放且重视创意时，在工作区比较少量实质不同的方案，通常两到三种。变化应涉及空间组织、字体层级、图像处理或解释方式。只换颜色属于同一方向的变体。比较时保持事实和页面任务一致；明显不合适的路线可用简短草案淘汰，需要看效果才能判断的方案再做渲染。

Use a representative body page and a difficult content page as the proof of the selected direction. A beautiful cover alone does not show that tables, citations, long Chinese labels, or a technical explanation will work. Select and proceed autonomously within the user's brief; show alternatives when requested or when the choice materially changes the assignment.

用有代表性的正文页和困难内容页验证选定方向。封面漂亮不能证明表格、引文、中文长标签和技术解释同样成立。在已授权需求内自主选择并推进；用户要求看方案，或选择会实质改变任务时再展示供决定。

Record only useful decisions in `design-plan.json`: concept, alternatives considered and their tradeoffs, selected direction, prototype pages, and the intended page sequence. Existing brand work can simply record the adopted rules. Keep this planning outside speaker notes.

在计划中简记概念、候选方案与取舍、选定方向、验证页及全稿节奏；品牌稿可只记录沿用规则。无需堆满固定字段，内部计划不放入演讲备注。

## Compose with intention / 有取舍地构图

| Decision / 决策 | Observable consequence / 可观察的结果 |
|---|---|
| Establish a focal point / 建立视觉中心 | The first glance finds the point, then a clear route to its evidence. / 第一眼找到主信息，再顺畅进入证据。 |
| Use scale and space expressively / 用尺度和空间表达 | A key figure, crop, diagram, or phrase earns its prominence; margins create grouping and tension. / 数字、图片、图解或语句因重要性而突出，留白形成分组与张力。 |
| Choose type for this voice and language / 按语气与语言选字 | Weight, line breaks, punctuation, CJK glyphs, and mixed-script baselines feel deliberate. / 字重、断行、标点、中日韩字形与混排基线经过处理。 |
| Give imagery a precise role / 为图像分配具体职责 | Framing and crop support the page's message; annotations relate to the actual object. / 构图与裁切支持表达，标注对应真实对象。 |
| Turn relationships into visual structure / 将关系变成结构 | Position, proximity, paths, or a chart explain what a paragraph otherwise has to describe. / 位置、远近、路径和图表帮助解释关系。 |
| Maintain rules while varying page roles / 统一规则，变化页面任务 | Reading, evidence, comparison, and transition pages create a useful rhythm. / 阅读、证据、比较和转场页形成有意义的节奏。 |

An asymmetric layout can still have rigorous alignment. A large photograph can coexist with editable text. A quiet page can contain a precise, memorable detail. Remove ornament that competes with meaning; retain expressive details that carry the subject or intended feeling. Do not replace every deck with the same monochrome, beige/teal, serif cover, or oversized number treatment.

非对称构图同样需要对齐；大图可以配原生文字；安静的页面也可以有准确而令人记住的细节。删掉争抢内容的装饰，保留承载主题与情绪的表达。不要把所有稿件洗成同一种黑白、米色配绿、衬线封面或巨型数字风格。

## Art-direct the asset / 将图片作为版面的一部分来设计

For generated imagery, define a subject-specific visual idea, image language, crop, and text-safe area before calling the host image tool. When image choice matters, inspect plausible candidates at their intended size and choose for relevance and composition. A single attractive image does not establish a coherent deck. Real product identity, documentary evidence, and data still require authentic sources.

生图前确定与主题相关的视觉想法、图像语言、裁切及文字安全区。关键配图需要在实际展示尺寸下比较相关性与构图。单张图片吸引人并不等于整稿形成风格；产品身份、纪实证据和数据仍需真实来源。

## Review craft and distinctiveness / 复查完成度与辨识度

After final-PPTX rendering, inspect the single pages and the deck sequence. Include concrete design observations in review summaries: what receives attention first, how the composition helps understanding, where typography/cropping still feels accidental, and whether repeated forms support the narrative. For a creativity brief, consider whether a neutral template has obscured the subject's particular qualities. A deliberately neutral report may still be the right answer for its audience.

最终 PPTX 渲染后，同时检查单页与全稿顺序。复查写具体观察：先看见什么、构图怎样帮助理解、哪处断行或裁切显得偶然、重复形式是否支持叙事。明确要求创意时，还应看模板是否掩盖了主题特点；面向相应受众的中性报告仍然可以成立。

Repair the most consequential weakness, then inspect the affected export again. When the brief is met, content is faithful, and visible craft problems are resolved, stop. Do not invent aesthetic scores, use “AI-looking” as authorship detection, or claim a technical validator certifies taste. Distinctiveness is a contextual design judgment; broad reliability needs varied tasks and human feedback.

优先修复影响最大的缺陷，再看新的导出结果。需求已满足、内容准确、可见完成度问题已解决时停止。不编造审美分数，不把“AI 味”用于鉴定作者，也不声称脚本能认证品位。辨识度属于具体语境下的设计判断，稳定性仍需多任务试用和人的反馈验证。
